Meow Lodge Data Deletion

App: Meow Lodge (냥이 하숙집) · Developer: K Chaebol Heavy Ind. · Effective: November 19, 2026

Meow Lodge has no accounts and stores no personal information such as names or emails on the developer's servers. You can delete your data as follows.

1. Game progress on your device. Open your device's Settings → Apps → Meow Lodge → Storage and tap Clear data. Uninstalling the app also deletes all progress stored on the device (rounds, stars, churu, cats, photos, settings) immediately.

2. Google Play Games records (from the version that adds rankings, only if you signed in). Open Google Play Games (or Play Store → profile → Play Games settings), go to Settings → Account & privacy → Delete Play Games account & data, and under Delete individual game data choose Meow Lodge.

3. Request by email. Send "Meow Lodge data deletion request" to elkardian2@gmail.com. We will handle it within 30 days and reply.

4. What is deleted and how long it is kept. Device progress: deleted immediately when you clear data or uninstall, not retained. Play Games rankings: kept in your Google account until you delete them as above. Ad data (Google AdMob): reset or delete your advertising ID in device Settings → Google → Ads; retained under Google's policies. The developer keeps no other copies except where required by law.
